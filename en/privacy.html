Website

Privacy

This is a static presentation website. It is designed to work without an account or marketing analytics tools.

Website data

BB16 Studio places no analytics cookie, advertising pixel or tracking SDK on this version of the website. Your light or dark mode choice is stored only in the browser, with no transmission to BB16 Studio. A hosting provider may process technical logs required for HTTPS delivery; those logs are governed by its own operation and public policies.

Tester feedback

The ConvertAir · Français and DocCipher · Français forms prepare feedback on your device. Responses are not sent to the studio automatically. Storage in your browser is optional; you can disable it or reset the form. You then choose whether to download the file or copy the summary, and send it to Lucas. If you send it, the studio receives the information you chose to provide in order to understand your feedback. Use an optional pseudonym and fictitious data; do not enter any PIN, password, secret or private document.

Contact

If you write to bb16studio@gmail.com, the team receives the information you choose to include so it can answer your message. Do not send a secret or sensitive document.

Applications

This page concerns the BB16 Studio website. The applications have their own data, third-party services and deletion flows. For ConvertAir, see ConvertAir privacy; for DocCipher, see DocCipher privacy. For HGQ, the policy published by the application is available on the HGQ privacy page · Français.

The website's HGQ deletion page · Français describes the known user flow and current contact channel.

Updates

This page will be updated when the legal identity, final hosting provider or website services change.
