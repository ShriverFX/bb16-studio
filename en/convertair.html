04 · Privacy

What stays with you.

Your documents remain on your phone

Scanning, conversions, signing and text recognition run on the device. BB16 Studio receives no copy.

Strictly limited Store services

Google Play processes the payment, and RevenueCat verifies Premium access. After a successful result is displayed, the official Google Play API may prompt for a review. No document content travels through these channels.

No advertising or audience measurement

No account, no advertising identifier, and no analytics SDK integrated into the application.

05 · Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

Are my documents sent to a server?

No. Processing runs on your phone. A file leaves ConvertAir only if you share it, save it elsewhere or print it.

What does the free version offer?

Three operations per day, processed on the device with no account. Premium removes the limit and export watermark, and adds batch processing.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In Google Play, at any time. Premium access remains active until the end of the paid period. The lifetime offer is a one-time payment, with no renewal.

Is an Internet connection required?

Not to process your documents. The connection is used for purchases and to verify Premium access.

When will ConvertAir be available?

The Android V1 is in preparation. The Google Play link will appear on this page as soon as it is published. An iOS version is being considered.
