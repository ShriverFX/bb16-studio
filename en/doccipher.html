Planned from launch · included with Premium

Two protections planned from launch.

Their local implementation is being validated. They will be offered at launch only if the required security reviews and on-device tests are conclusive. DocCipher is not yet available on Google Play.

Panic code

A Premium option that you enable voluntarily with your master password, a separate PIN entered twice, two consent steps and confirmation of the irreversible action. You choose selected vaults or all vaults.

The armed PIN triggers logical deletion of the files, local keys, caches and thumbnails of the selected vaults: ‘Delete the vaults on this device. Your external backups are not deleted.’ Only the exact entry of the armed panic PIN triggers this action. The operation does not promise physical erasure of the phone's storage.

Decoy vault

A separate local vault, with its own PIN, keys and content, to open if someone is looking over your shoulder or asks you to open the application.

The feature is documented publicly: it protects against casual observation, but does not guarantee that an observer is unaware of its existence or that device analysis cannot distinguish it.

Lifetime Premium entitlement includes both features at no extra cost.

04 · Privacy

What stays with you.

Encrypted on the device

Documents, previews, recognised text and vault data remain encrypted in the application's private space.

No account or document server

BB16 Studio does not host, synchronise or back up any document. Your documents leave only through an export you initiate.

No documents in third-party services

Google Play and RevenueCat manage offers, purchases, restorations and Premium access. After viewing offers or making a purchase, checks may also take place when the app is unlocked and when you return to it. No document, vault, password, PIN or key passes through these processes.

05 · Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

What if I forget my password?

Your master password is neither stored nor sent: no one, not even BB16 Studio, can reset it. The recovery kit (phrase and QR code), which you can print or save, is provided for this purpose.

Are my documents backed up online?

No. You export an encrypted .dcvault backup yourself to a location of your choice. WebDAV or Nextcloud synchronisation is not available in this version.

Is Premium a subscription?

No: 19,99 € once. Without a purchase, DocCipher remains usable for up to 15 documents.

Is an Internet connection required?

The vault runs on the device. An Internet connection is required to view current offers, purchase Premium and restore purchases. Offline Premium access relies on entitlements already recorded on this device.

What data is used for purchases?

No BB16 account is required. RevenueCat processes a pseudonymous identifier, technical information and purchase history to manage Premium and sales statistics. It does not receive your documents.

Will the panic code and decoy vault be available at launch?

They are planned from launch and included in the lifetime Premium entitlement at no extra cost. Their local implementation is still being validated: they will be made available only if the required security reviews and on-device tests are conclusive.

When will DocCipher be available?

DocCipher is coming soon to Android. It is not yet available on Google Play; the link will appear on this page after publication.
