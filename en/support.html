Help and feedback

Support

One public address for product questions, access problems and data-related requests.

bb16studio@gmail.com

To receive a useful answer

  • State which application is concerned: ConvertAir, DocCipher or HGQ.
  • Describe the behaviour you observed and the device or version, if you know them.
  • Never attach a password, private key, recovery phrase, vault file or personal document.

For HGQ deletion, first use the flow in the application. If you have lost access, the deletion page · Français explains the minimum information to provide.

Product feedback

Ideas and reports are read according to the team's capacity and the project's state. Receiving a message does not amount to a promise of a feature, deadline or publication.
