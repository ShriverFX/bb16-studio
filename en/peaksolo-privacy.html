PeakSolo · in preparation

PeakSolo privacy policy

Preparatory policy: PeakSolo has not been published. Current limitations and planned processing are distinguished below; this policy will be finalised before release.

At a glance

  • PeakSolo is in preparation; this policy is not a release approval.
  • The project aims to keep business data on the device.
  • Planned purchases go through Google Play and RevenueCat.
  • Backup through user-initiated export; Google components still need on-device verification.
  • Future regulatory submissions will have to be confirmed one by one.
On this page
  1. Business data and storage
  2. Purchases and Premium access
  3. Limitations of the version in preparation
  4. Future exports and transmissions
  5. Your requests

Business data and storage

The code in preparation uses an encrypted SQLCipher database for business data. Legacy Isar databases and files outside the database still need separate handling. Migration, storage and keys must still be verified on a device.

Purchases and Premium access

Google Play and RevenueCat are planned to display offers, make purchases, restore purchases and verify Premium access. This may transmit a pseudonymous identifier, technical information, the IP address, and purchase tokens and history. Purchase amounts for the application are used for its commercial management.

Customers, invoices and amounts from your business must not be sent to RevenueCat. Actual traffic and purchase configuration still need to be verified before release.

Limitations of the version in preparation

The current backup is an encrypted file exported at your request through the system file picker. There is no built-in Drive connection or automatic synchronization in this flow. If you choose an external service, its rules apply. OCR uses on-device ML Kit; communications by Google components still need to be checked before release.

Future exports and transmissions

An export to another application or service you choose is also subject to its rules. Future regulatory transmissions will have to name the recipient and ask for your confirmation for each submission. Hiding the explanation must never remove the confirmation. This workflow is not announced as available.

Your requests

For any question about your data, contact BB16 Studio at bb16studio@gmail.com. Do not send any private or secret document with your request.

The full legal identity, retention periods and final provider settings will be specified before the application is published.
